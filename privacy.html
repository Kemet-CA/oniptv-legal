ON IPTV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

ON IPTV, developed by Kemet CA (“the app,” “we,” “our”) is a media player for a streaming source that you provide. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Summary

ON IPTV does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us or to any third party. We operate no servers, offer no user accounts, and use no analytics or advertising.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.
  • We operate no backend server that receives your data.
  • We do not sell or share any data — we have none to sell or share.

Information stored on your device

To function, the app stores the following only on your Roku device, in its local app storage:

  • The source connection details you enter (for example, an Xtream server, username and password, or an M3U playlist URL).
  • Your preferences: favorites, continue-watching positions, selected profile, interface language, and an optional parental PIN.

This information never leaves your device except as necessary to connect directly to the source you provided (for example, your own Xtream or M3U server) so the app can play the content you request. That connection is between your device and the provider you chose; we are not involved and do not receive it.

Content

ON IPTV includes no channels, playlists, movies, or television. All content is supplied by the source you connect and is delivered directly from that source to your device. We do not host, provide, index, control, or endorse any content. You are solely responsible for the source you use and for complying with all applicable laws and rights.

Children

ON IPTV is a general-purpose media player and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because your data is stored only on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting a profile in the app, using Delete all local data in Settings, or removing the channel from your Roku device.

Third parties

The app connects only to the source you configure. It integrates no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date above.
