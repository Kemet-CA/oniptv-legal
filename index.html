What it is

ON IPTV is a premium media player. It ships with no channels, playlists, movies, or TV — you connect a source you are legally authorized to access, and enjoy it in a fast, modern interface built for the TV remote. Think of it like a web browser or a media player: the app is the tool; the content is yours.
